Home screen widgets

Five widgets in the same "Pure" style as the app. Mode buttons are tappable here, like on the phone (switches mode without opening the app). Swipe between wallpapers and states.

Sizes are Pixel launcher cells: 2×2, 4×1, 4×2, 2×1. Every widget is resizable within its family. When the headphones are off, widgets dim and show the last known battery with "Not connected"; tapping opens the app.
